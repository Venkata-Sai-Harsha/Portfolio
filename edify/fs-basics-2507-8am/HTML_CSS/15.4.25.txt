Lists: Collection of similar html elements displayed in web page.

Types:
1. Ordered List: Proper order of every element.
2. Unordered List: Will have symbolic representation for every element.

1. Ordered List:

    Syntax:

        <ol type="1 | A | a | I | i">
            <li>Content</li>
            <li>Content</li>
            <li>Content</li>
        </ol>

        ol - ordered list
        li - list item

2. Unordered List:

    Syntax:

        <ul>
            <li>Content</li>
            <li>Content</li>
            <li>Content</li>
        </ul>
        

        CSS:
        list-style-type: disc | circle | square | none;
