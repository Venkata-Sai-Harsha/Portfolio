Color Codes:

    color: colorname | Hex Codes | RGB | HSL;

    Hex Codes:

        #000000 - #FFFFFF (Hexa Decimal Format)

        00 - First two values - Red
        00 - Second two values - Green
        00 - Third two values - Blue


        #0b57d0

    RGB: 

        rgb(0, 0, 0) to rgb(255, 255, 255)

    HSL:

        Syntax:

            hsl(0, 50%, 50%);

            0 - 360

                (0 - 120 - Red, 121 - 240 - Green, 241 - 360 - Blue)
 
            0% - 100%

        H - Hue - Color 
        S - Saturation - Increase the intensity of the color.
        L - Lightness - Decrease the intensity of the color.
